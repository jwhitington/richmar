Common questions

Before you commit, here's what most people ask.

How much does a whole-home Generac cost installed?

Most Oakwood-area homes land between $8,000 and $14,000 fully installed — generator, transfer switch, concrete pad, gas line, permits, and inspection. The right number depends on your load and your gas supply. We'll quote it in writing after a free on-site visit, with no pressure.

What size generator do I actually need?

"As big as possible" is bad advice. Most three- and four-bedroom homes run beautifully on a 14–22 kW air-cooled unit. We size to your real loads — HVAC tonnage, well pump, sump, oven — not to a square-footage rule of thumb.

Natural gas, propane, or both?

If you have natural gas at the meter, that's almost always the right call — no tank, no refills, no run-time limit. If you're rural with no gas, we run on propane and right-size your tank to your worst-case outage.

How loud is it when running?

About as loud as a central A/C condenser at typical setback distance. It also self-tests weekly for ~5 minutes — we schedule that for midday, not 2am, so your dog doesn't file a complaint.

Do you service generators we didn't sell?

Yes. If your Generac was installed by someone else (or is out of warranty), we can pick up service. We'll do a baseline inspection first so we both know what we're starting with.
