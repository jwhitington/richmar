Installation

Turn-key install. You answer the door once.

We coordinate the permit, the gas line, the pad, the electrical, and the inspection so you don't have to be the project manager.
DAY 0

On-site assessment

Free visit. We measure loads, scope gas & electrical, and pick a unit location that's quiet & serviceable.

DAY 1–3

Permit & order

We pull local permits and order your unit and ATS direct from Generac.

DAY 4

Pad & gas

Concrete pad poured and cured. Gas line run and pressure-tested to code.

DAY 5–6

Set & wire

Generator lands on the pad. Transfer switch wired into your main panel. Mobile Link™ paired to your phone.

DAY 7

Inspect & commission

Local inspection, simulated outage, run-test, and a walkthrough so you know exactly what it does.
