# Richmar Energies — Image Sourcing Guide

Free, high-quality, royalty-free photo suggestions for every section of the site. All searches work on Unsplash.com, Pexels.com, and StockSnap.io unless noted. Look for images that feel **warm, residential, midwestern, and human** — not stock-photo-corporate.

## Universal rules
- **Aspect ratio:** Crop to 5:4 or 4:3 for editorial blocks, 1:1 for portraits, 16:9 for hero rows.
- **License:** Confirm "free to use, no attribution required" on Unsplash & Pexels. Generac product photography should come from the **Generac Dealer Media Kit** (you have access as an authorized dealer — log in to dealer.generac.com).
- **Watermark check:** Don't grab a watermarked SKU shot from a retailer's website.
- **Avoid:** Photos with logos of competitors or unrelated brands in frame.
