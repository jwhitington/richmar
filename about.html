Working with Richmar

What to expect, in plain English.

How fast can you get out for a quote?

Usually within a week, faster outside of storm season. We bring a clipboard, look at your panel, your gas meter, and your lot, and you'll have a written quote within a few business days.

Do I have to be a Generac person to work with you?

We sell and service Generac because we're certified on it and because parts and warranty support are the best in class for our area. If you've got a different brand and need a one-time service call, ask — sometimes we can help, sometimes we'll refer.

What if I'm just outside the 50-mile radius?

Call anyway. We make exceptions for the right project, especially if you're a few miles past the line or part of a community we're already serving.

Is financing available?

Yes — through Generac's preferred finance partners, subject to approval. We can walk you through monthly payment estimates during your quote visit.
